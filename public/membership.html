Before it hurts

See it while you can still change it.

Gum inflammation, a narrowing airway, bone thinning under a tooth — almost none of it hurts at first. By the time something is uncomfortable, the choices left are fewer and more expensive. All of it is visible in your mouth long before you would ever feel it.

The mouth is not a sixth thing to track. It is upstream of the other five.

Insurance

What if I already have dental insurance?

Then keep it. Membership is not a replacement for your plan and it is not insurance — it is the half your plan was never built to cover. We help you use every dollar of your benefit before it resets, and membership picks up everything after that.

What your plan is built for

Fixing what already broke

  • Two cleanings and exams a year, on the calendar rather than on your risk
  • X-rays on a fixed schedule, and about seventeen cents on the dollar toward a 3D scan
  • A share of fillings, crowns and root canals
  • An annual maximum — reset each year whether or not you used it
  • Nothing at all toward whitening, cosmetic work, night guards, panels or products
What membership adds

Seeing it before it breaks

  • A 3D scan and an oral cancer screening every year, and one more preventive visit than your plan funds
  • Your Primary iD, re-scored every year and read at every visit
  • Member pricing with no maximum — nobody tells you you have used too much
  • The categories your plan ignores — whitening, cosmetic, aligners, guards, panels, products
  • Unlimited second opinions, including on plans from other dentists

The two work together. Bring your card to the first visit. We’ll tell you what your plan covers, what it doesn’t, and what your benefit is worth before the end of the year. Then membership picks up the rest — and every dollar it saves you on whitening, cosmetic work, a night guard or a panel is a dollar no dental plan would ever have saved you.
